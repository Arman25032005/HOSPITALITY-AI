# Stay Policies

> DEMONSTRATION CONTENT: fictional hotel policies for product demos only.

## Check-in and Check-out
Standard check-in time at Demo Grand Hotel is 3:00 PM. Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Photo identification and a payment card are required at check-in.

Early check-in from 12:00 PM is subject to room availability and is not guaranteed. Guests may store luggage with the front desk free of charge before check-in and after check-out.

## Late Checkout
Late checkout is subject to availability and requires approval from the front desk. Checkout until 1:00 PM is complimentary for loyalty members. Checkout until 4:00 PM costs 50 USD. Departures after 4:00 PM are charged a full night at the room rate. Requests must be made before 9:00 PM the evening prior to departure where possible.

## Cancellation Policy
Reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the 3:00 PM check-in time on the arrival date. Cancellations made within 48 hours are charged the first night. A no-show is charged the first night and the remainder of the reservation is released.

## Pets
Dogs under 25 pounds are welcome in designated pet-friendly rooms for a fee of 50 USD per stay. Pets are not permitted in the Terrace Restaurant or the spa. Service animals are welcome everywhere in the hotel at no charge.

## Smoking
Demo Grand Hotel is a smoke-free property. A cleaning fee of 250 USD applies to rooms where smoking is detected.
