# Dining

> DEMONSTRATION CONTENT: fictional restaurant details for product demos only.

## Terrace Restaurant Breakfast
Breakfast is served at the Terrace Restaurant. Weekday hours are 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM. Weekend and holiday hours are 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM. The breakfast buffet costs 24 USD per adult and 12 USD per child aged 5 to 12. Children under 5 eat free. Breakfast is complimentary for guests staying in an Executive Suite.

## Dinner
The Terrace Restaurant serves dinner from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM. Reservations are recommended on weekends. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available on request.

## Lobby Bar
The Lobby Bar is open from 4:00 PM to midnight daily.
