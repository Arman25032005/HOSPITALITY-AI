# Hotel Amenities

> DEMONSTRATION CONTENT: fictional amenities for product demos only.

## Wi-Fi
Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel on the network DemoGrand-Guest. Premium high-speed Wi-Fi for streaming and video conferencing is available for 10 USD per day. The Wi-Fi access code is provided at check-in.

## Parking
Self-parking in the covered garage costs 25 USD per night. Valet parking costs 40 USD per night. Four electric vehicle charging stations are located on garage level 1 and are available on a first-come basis at no additional charge. Oversized vehicles over 6.5 feet in height cannot enter the garage.

## Rooftop Pool
The rooftop pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Towels are provided poolside. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. There is no lifeguard on duty.

## Fitness Center
The fitness center on the second floor is open 24 hours and is accessed with your room key card.

## Room Service
In-room dining is available daily from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. A delivery fee of 5 USD applies to each order.

## Laundry
Same-day laundry service is available for items received at the front desk before 9:00 AM. Pricing is listed in the guest directory.
