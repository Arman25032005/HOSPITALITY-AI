# Room Types

> DEMONSTRATION CONTENT: fictional room descriptions for product demos only.

## Standard Queen
Sleeps up to 2 guests with one queen bed, a work desk and city views. Rates start at 150 USD per night.

## Deluxe King
Sleeps up to 3 guests with one king bed and a sofa bed, a larger work area and a rain shower. Rates start at 220 USD per night.

## Executive Suite
Sleeps up to 4 guests with a separate living room, a king bed, a sofa bed and a soaking tub. Rates start at 380 USD per night. Complimentary breakfast is included.
