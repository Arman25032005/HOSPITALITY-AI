# Internal Guest Recovery Guidelines

> DEMONSTRATION CONTENT: fictional internal staff procedure. CONFIDENTIAL: not for guests.

## Service Recovery Authority
Front desk staff may offer a goodwill credit of up to 50 USD without approval. Credits above 50 USD require approval from the duty manager. Never promise compensation before approval is recorded.

## Emergencies
For medical, fire or safety emergencies, staff must call the emergency number immediately and notify the duty manager. Automated assistants must not give medical or safety instructions beyond directing the guest to call emergency services and alerting staff.
